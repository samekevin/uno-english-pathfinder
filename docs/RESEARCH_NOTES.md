# Research notes and editorial decisions

This research pass was assembled for the UNO English Pathfinder Explore English graph on 2026-10-03. It prioritizes current official UNO pages. Faculty personal/research sites are included only when the UNO profile itself links to them.

## Active faculty scope

The current English directory contains the department chair plus 26 people in the English Department Faculty section before the Emeritus heading: 27 active faculty nodes total. Emeritus faculty are deliberately excluded from the active-faculty graph even when older concentration pages still display them.

## Known title discrepancies

The English directory's role labels lag several newer official university promotion announcements. The graph stores both the directory value and a newer current role where there is clear official evidence. Notable examples include Jody Keisner (Professor), Melanie Seitzer (Lecturer), and the 2026 promotions of Rachel Bash, Sarah Mason, Jillian Quandt, and Eric O'Brien to Lecturer.

## Current SoLaS research nodes

As verified on 2026-10-03, SoLaS lists **English Spoken in Omaha** and **English and Cebuano Onomatopoeias** as current projects, both with ongoing recruitment. Its student page says research-assistant positions are often paid depending on funding, UNO students are typically enrolled through the English internship program, and volunteer research internships are open to UNO/non-UNO undergraduate and graduate students with a stated minimum five-hour weekly commitment. Kevin Samejon is listed as director and Sarah Faltin Osborn as affiliate faculty.

## Bouteloua 2026 snapshot

The 2026 Bouteloua LitART Fest is scheduled for **October 4, 2026, 2:00–6:00 PM CDT at Glacier Creek Preserve**. Ellen Forney is the keynote; participating authors include Tammie Kennedy; The Linden Review sponsors the “Writing about Illness & Health” panel. ## Fast-changing items

Anything involving application windows, current event dates, grant cycles, internship openings, and recurring study-abroad offerings should be re-verified before production. The graph stores these as availability metadata rather than presenting old dates as permanent facts.
